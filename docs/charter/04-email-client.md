# The Ideal Email Client

_Charter #4 (formerly issue #4)._

What email has to do to belong inside a single trusted system rather than living in a separate app.

## Hard requirements

_To be detailed._

Prompts worth answering: Is mail stored in the vault or only mirrored? What survives offline? How does a message become a task, an event, or a note without copy-paste? What does triage look like at 500 messages? What may an agent draft, and what must never be sent without a human? How do threads relate to projects and contacts?

## Current state

Read-and-draft only: accounts can be listed, envelopes and messages read, replies and new messages drafted into Drafts. There is deliberately no send path. An email watcher exists as a nix service.
