# The Ideal Client-Facing Deliverable Preview

_Charter #14 (formerly issue #14)._

What a client, collaborator or band member sees when you send them work — and what they can do with it without an account, an app, or a phone call.

## Hard requirements

_To be detailed._

Prompts worth answering: What does a reviewer see on a link, and what must they never see? How do timestamped comments work across audio, video, images, PDFs and documents? How are versions presented — can they compare, and against what? What does approval mean, and is it binding? What happens to comments when a new version lands? What does the sender learn (viewed, downloaded, approved)? How much works on a phone, offline, or in a bad browser?

## Non-goals

_To be detailed._

## Current state

Share links with capabilities (comment / download / file-request), password, expiry, reversible disable and an org-wide kill switch; an anonymous guest RPC lane scoped to a review; a review player with version switching, compare, frame-anchored annotation and a comment rail; proxy renditions served when download is withheld; an access log recording view/browse/rendition/download.
