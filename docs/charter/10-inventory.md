# The Ideal Inventory System

_Charter #10 (formerly issue #10)._

Physical things — gear, instruments, cables, drives — tracked well enough to answer "where is it, whose is it, and does it work".

## Hard requirements

_To be detailed._

Prompts worth answering: What is an item versus a model versus a unit? How is location tracked without constant manual updates? What happens when gear goes out on a job and comes back — or does not? How do serial numbers, purchase records, warranties and insurance values attach? What does maintenance history look like? How does inventory connect to a session, a project, or a room?
