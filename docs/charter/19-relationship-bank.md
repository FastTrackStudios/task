# The Relationship Bank

_Charter #19 (formerly issue #19)._

People, and what the system owes you about them. The framing is deliberate: relationships accrue and deplete, and a system that only stores contact details is a rolodex, not a bank.

## Hard requirements

_To be detailed._

Prompts worth answering: What is recorded about an interaction, and how much of it can be captured without manual entry? What does the system tell you *before* a meeting, and what does it ask you after? How is an obligation to someone tracked — a promise made, a favour owed, a reply outstanding? What surfaces someone you have not spoken to in too long, and how does it decide "too long"? How do clients differ from collaborators, family, and acquaintances? What does a relationship carry across orgs? What is deliberately never inferred about a person? What happens to their data when the relationship ends?

## Non-goals

_To be detailed._

## Current state

Contacts exist as vault entities with per-org paths and frontmatter, plus a contacts-interaction surface and contact chips rendered inline in the editor. There is no obligation tracking, no resurfacing, and no notion of relationship health.
