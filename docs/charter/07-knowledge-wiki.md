# The Ideal Knowledge Wiki

_Charter #7 (formerly issue #7)._

The curated, synthesised layer above raw notes — what the system has *learned*, as opposed to what you happened to write down.

## Hard requirements

_To be detailed._

Prompts worth answering: What distinguishes a wiki page from a vault note? Who writes it — human, agent, or both — and how is provenance recorded? How does a claim get challenged or retired when it stops being true? What makes a page trustworthy enough to act on? How does it stay in sync with the code and notes it describes?
