## Rules

1. **Preserve the existing frontmatter** (`type`, `title`, `created`, `tags`, `related`, `sources`, `folder`) but bump `updated` to today's date.
2. **Length: target 300–800 words.** Single-paragraph pages are a failure.

6. **Keep `sources:` traceability** — never remove a source from the frontmatter array.
