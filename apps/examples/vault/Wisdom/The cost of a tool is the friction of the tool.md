# The cost of a tool is the friction of the tool

A productivity tool's real cost isn't the price or the learning
curve — it's the cumulative micro-friction of using it every
day. A todo app that takes 4 taps to capture a task imposes a
tax on every thought you have.

Implication for [[Task]]: capture must be one keystroke from
anywhere, or it doesn't matter how clever the rest is. Editing
later is fine. Capture has to be free.

Related: [[Habit stacking]] (rituals stack on low-friction triggers).
