# Math reference

Math compiles via Typst. Inline forms sit in body text;
display forms live on their own line.

## Inline

The familiar identity: $e^(i pi) + 1 = 0$. The Pythagorean
theorem: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$. Stirling's approximation:
$n! ~ sqrt(2 pi n) (n/e)^n$.

## Display

$$ integral_(-oo)^(oo) e^(-x^2) d x = sqrt(pi) $$

$$ sum_(k=1)^n k = n(n+1)/2 $$

$$ d/(d x) integral_a^x f(t) d t = f(x) $$
