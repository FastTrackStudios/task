steps:
- Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Drop the spaghetti.
- While the pasta cooks, warm the olive oil in a wide pan over medium-low heat. Add the sliced garlic and red pepper flakes; cook until the garlic is just blonde, ~2 min. Don't brown it.
- Reserve a cup of pasta water; drain the spaghetti when al dente.
- Toss the pasta in the garlic oil. Splash in pasta water a tablespoon at a time, tossing, until the sauce clings.
- Off heat, fold in the parmesan. Plate and finish with more cheese.

The technique is [[mise en place]] before the water boils — there's no time to slice
garlic once the pasta hits the pan. Lean on [[saute]] cues (sizzle steady, no
crackle) for the garlic; brown garlic is bitter.

For a richer version, swap a third of the olive oil for butter at the toss step.
