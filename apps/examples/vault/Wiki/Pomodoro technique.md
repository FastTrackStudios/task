# Pomodoro technique

Time management method: work in 25-minute focused intervals
("pomodoros") separated by 5-minute breaks. After 4 pomodoros,
take a 15-30 minute long break.

The rule is **non-negotiable interruption**: if the timer is
running, you don't context-switch. If a distraction arrives
(idea, slack ping, hunger), write it on a scratchpad and
return to it on the next break.

Pomodoros are coarse-grained estimation units: "this feature
will take 6 pomodoros" is more honest than "this feature will
take 3 hours" because pomodoros include the friction.
