# Andrej Karpathy

Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher and educator associated with influential public explanations of neural networks, LLMs, and AI software workflows. In this source, he is relevant as the author of the LLM Wiki design note.

The note frames LLM-assisted knowledge management as a persistent maintenance problem rather than a one-off question answering problem. Its proposal is that an LLM can act as a wiki maintainer: ingesting sources, updating pages, filing answers, and linting the resulting knowledge base over time.

Related concepts include [[LLM Wiki]], [[Retrieval-Augmented Generation]], and persistent synthesis in local-first markdown knowledge systems.
