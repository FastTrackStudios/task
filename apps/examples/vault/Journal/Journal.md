# Journal

Dated, human-written notes. Distinct from `[[Records]]` (machine-emitted
history) — Journal entries are reflection and synthesis you write.

- `[[Daily]]` — `YYYY-MM-DD.md` daily synthesis.
- `[[Meetings]]` — `YYYY-MM-DD <topic>.md` meeting notes.
