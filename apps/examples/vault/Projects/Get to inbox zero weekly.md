# Get to inbox zero weekly

Sunday evening sweep: archive, reply, file. Goal is "no email
older than 7 days in the inbox at 8pm Sunday."

Process:
1. Triage — archive newsletters, file receipts.
2. Reply to anything that takes <2 minutes.
3. For longer replies, capture a task with `task capture "reply
   to <person> re: <topic>"`.

Related: [[The cost of a tool is the friction of the tool]] —
inbox is a tool whose friction compounds if you don't clear it.
