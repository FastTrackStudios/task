# People

Personal relationships and contacts — people you actually know or
collaborate with. In an org-scope vault, this is staff and external
contacts.

**Not for public figures or historical influences.** Those live in
`[[Wiki/entities]]` as concept-shape entries (e.g. `andrej-karpathy`,
`bret-victor`, `linus-torvalds`, `marijn-haverbeke`).
