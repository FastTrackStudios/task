## Quotes and callouts

> A quote. Press Return and it continues;
> press Return on an empty line to leave it.

> [!note] A note callout
> Callouts are quotes with a type.

> [!tip] A tip
> Try `[!warning]`, `[!example]`, `[!question]`, `[!success]` and more.

> [!warning]+ A collapsible callout
> The `+` or `-` after the type folds it open or closed.

> [!example] Callouts nest
> > [!info] Inside another
> > Add a `>` for each level.
