# Deep work

Sustained, distraction-free concentration on a cognitively
demanding task. Cal Newport's coinage; opposed to **shallow
work** (email, meetings, busywork that doesn't compound).

Key practices:
- **Schedule blocks** of 1-4 hours, calendar them as
  appointments with yourself.
- **Phone in another room.** Notifications off. Browser
  closed if you're not using it.
- **Track the metric.** Hours of deep work per week is a
  leading indicator of output.

Related: [[Pomodoro technique]], [[Habit stacking]].
