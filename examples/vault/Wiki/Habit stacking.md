# Habit stacking

Technique for installing a new habit by chaining it to an
existing one: **"after I [existing habit], I will [new habit]"**.
The existing habit becomes the trigger; you don't need to
remember the new one separately.

Examples:
- After I pour my morning coffee, I will write three things I'm
  grateful for.
- After I brush my teeth, I will do 10 push-ups.
- After I close my laptop for the day, I will lay out clothes
  for tomorrow.

The stack scales: chain several habits together and the whole
sequence becomes one composite routine (see [[Morning routine]]
for an example). The earliest habit in the stack does the
heavy lifting — it triggers the rest.

Related: [[Spaced repetition]], [[Zettelkasten]], [[Pomodoro technique]].
