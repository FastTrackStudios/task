# Records

Append-only history. Things that happened, are scheduled, or were
emitted by a tool. Distinct from `[[Journal]]` (reflection you write).

- `bookings/` — scheduled and historical bookings. Emitted by the
  `scheduling` crate (`features/scheduling/scheduling`).

Future: time entries, audit trails, completed agent-task receipts.
