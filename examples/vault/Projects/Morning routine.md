# Morning routine

Stacked habits run between waking and starting work. Goal: get
out of bed and into focused work in 60 minutes without
willpower spent on what's next.

## The stack

Each step triggers the next ([[Habit stacking]]):

1. **Wake** (alarm 6:30) → drink full glass of water
2. → Bathroom: brush, floss, shave (alternate days)
3. → Shower (cold-finish, see [[2026-05-21 cold showers]])
4. → Get dressed (clothes laid out night before)
5. → Make bed
6. → Coffee + 10min reading (no screens)
7. → 10min meditation
8. → First pomodoro of deep work ([[Deep work]])

## Tracked tasks

See `tasks/` — every step above has a recurring task
(`recurrence: "FREQ=DAILY"`) that I check off in the morning.
