# Purpose

The research behind Alice’s Bible Study: archived sources (videos,
books, articles) with their summaries and timestamped notes, and the
topics, passages, questions, people, words and study paths an agent has
written from them.

This is a library, not a conclusion. An agent writes it, broadly; Alice
reads it, and when she has studied a page enough she takes it into her
own **Bible Study** (`bible-study`), where it becomes hers to rewrite.
The library page stays, and says where its copy went.

## Scope

In: anything a source says about scripture, its setting and its
reception, laid out to be read fast (`skills/wiki-style.md`) and cited
to the exact place.

Out: Alice’s own conclusions — those live in her Bible Study.

## Standards

- Every claim from a source cites the moment or page it comes from.
- Pages that rest on one voice say so (the wiki lists them); a second,
  independent source is always the next thing to look for.
