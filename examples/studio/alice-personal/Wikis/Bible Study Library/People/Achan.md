# Achan

The Israelite who takes from Jericho’s [[Herem|ḥērem]] — a Babylonian cloak, two hundred shekels of silver and a bar of gold — and hides them under his tent ([[bible::Josh.7.21|Joshua 7:21]]). Israel is routed at Ai until it is found out ([[bible::Josh.7.1-Josh.7.26|Joshua 7:1–26]]).

He is the illustration of what [[Herem]] means: the goods were not evil, but they had been handed to God, and he took them back into human use [[god-told-them-to-kill-everyone-here-s-why-76b70b2b#^t1742|29:02]]. In [[The Kirta Epic]] parallel he stands where Kirta’s broken vow stands.
