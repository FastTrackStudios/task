# Goliath

The Philistine champion “of Gath” ([[bible::1Sam.17.4|1 Samuel 17:4]]) who challenges Israel in the Valley of Elah. His bronze coat weighs 5,000 shekels (about 125 lb) and his spear’s iron head 600 shekels (about 15 lb) ([[bible::1Sam.17.5-1Sam.17.7|1 Samuel 17:5–7]]).

## Why Gath matters

Gath is one of the three cities where Anakim survived Joshua’s campaign ([[bible::Josh.11.22|Joshua 11:22]]). Read in sequence, Goliath is not a random big man but the unfinished business of the conquest, four centuries on [[god-told-them-to-kill-everyone-here-s-why-76b70b2b#^t2834|47:14]].

David, in this reading, is the new Joshua. His men then kill four more giants of Gath, among them a brother of Goliath ([[bible::2Sam.21.19|2 Samuel 21:19]]).
