# Principalities and Powers

Paul’s rulers, authorities, powers, thrones and dominions are the [[Divine Council]] in Greek — specifically its second tier, the sons of God given the nations in [[Deuteronomy 32]], who took them for themselves. Paul does not argue for this framework; he assumes it [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t2596|43:16]].

## What Paul says about them

Each passage in full, with what it adds to the picture.

[[bible::Eph.6.12|Ephesians 6:12]] — The fullest list of the powers in one place — rulers, authorities, cosmic powers, spiritual forces — and the claim that they, not people, are the real opponents [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t2596|43:16]].

[[bible::Col.1.16|Colossians 1:16]] — They were **created**, through Christ and for him: the council’s members are part of creation, not rivals to its maker [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t1682|28:02]].

[[bible::Col.2.15|Colossians 2:15]] — The cross as a victory over them: disarmed and paraded, as a conquered king’s officers were in a Roman triumph.

[[bible::1Cor.2.8|1 Corinthians 2:8]] — They engineered the crucifixion and misread it; had they understood, they would not have done it [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t2687|44:47]].

[[bible::1Cor.15.24-1Cor.15.25|1 Corinthians 15:24–25]] — The end of the story: every rule, authority and power destroyed, and the kingdom handed to the Father.

[[bible::Gal.4.8|Galatians 4:8]] — Gentiles before the gospel served beings “that by nature are not gods” — the nations’ allotted rulers of [[Deuteronomy 32]].

[[bible::Gal.4.3|Galatians 4:3]] — The [[Stoicheia|stoicheia]], “elemental spirits”: probably the same powers under another name [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t2734|45:34]]; compare [[bible::Col.2.8|Colossians 2:8]].

[[bible::Eph.3.10|Ephesians 3:10]] — The church itself is the announcement: its spread through the nations tells the powers their hold is broken [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t3146|52:26]].

## The shape of it

- **Created, so answerable.** Colossians 1:16 puts them in the created order under Christ — the same point [[Does the Bible teach that other gods exist]] makes from Psalm 82 [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t1682|28:02]].
- **Outwitted at the cross.** The rulers of this age engineered the crucifixion thinking it a victory; it was the moment their claim was broken [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t2687|44:47]].
- **Gentiles were their subjects.** The Galatians, before the gospel, lived under the administration of the beings assigned their nation at Babel [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t2734|45:34]].
- **The church is the announcement.** Ephesians 3:10 makes the church, spread through the nations, the notice served on the powers that their nations are being taken back [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t3146|52:26]]. See [[Reversing Babel]].
