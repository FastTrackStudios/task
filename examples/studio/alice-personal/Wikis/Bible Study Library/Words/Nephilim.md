# Nĕpilîm

Twice in the Bible: born of the sons of God and human women, “in those days, and also afterward” ([[bible::Gen.6.4|Genesis 6:4]]), and seen by the spies in Canaan, whose Anakim “come from the Nephilim” ([[bible::Num.13.33|Numbers 13:33]]). Often derived from *nāpal*, to fall; others take it as a borrowed word for giants. See [[Nephilim and Anakim]] and [[Watchers and Demons]].
