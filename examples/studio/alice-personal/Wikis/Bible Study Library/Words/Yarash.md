# Yārash

*To take possession; to dispossess.* It is the verb for inheriting land ([[bible::Deut.1.8|Deuteronomy 1:8]]) and for driving its holders out ([[bible::Deut.9.3|Deuteronomy 9:3]]). Most conquest commands use it, not [[Herem|ḥērem]] [[god-told-them-to-kill-everyone-here-s-why-76b70b2b#^t911|15:11]]. See [[Verbs of Conquest]].
