# Stoicheia

The things a row is made of — letters of an alphabet, elements of the world, the basics of a subject. In [[bible::Gal.4.3|Galatians 4:3]] and [[bible::Col.2.8|Colossians 2:8]] Paul says the Gentiles were enslaved to them; set beside “beings that by nature are not gods” ([[bible::Gal.4.8|Galatians 4:8]]), many read spiritual powers rather than ideas [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t2734|45:34]].
