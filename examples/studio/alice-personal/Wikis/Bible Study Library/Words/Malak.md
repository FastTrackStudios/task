# Malʾāk

*Messenger.* Jacob sends *malʾākîm* to Esau ([[bible::Gen.32.3|Genesis 32:3]]); the same word names the beings who carry God’s errands. In the three tiers of the [[Divine Council]] they are the third: not rulers but envoys [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t682|11:22]].
