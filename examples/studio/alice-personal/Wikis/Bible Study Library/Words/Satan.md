# Śāṭān

*Adversary.* A human can be one ([[bible::1Kgs.11.14|1 Kings 11:14]]). In [[bible::Job.1.6|Job 1:6]] the word has the article — *ha-śāṭān*, “the accuser” — and names a function in the [[Divine Council]]: the one who challenges Yahweh’s verdict on Job [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t909|15:09]]. The personal enemy of the New Testament grows from here.
