# Does the Bible teach that other gods exist?

## The short answer

Yes — other spiritual beings, which the Hebrew Bible calls [[Elohim]], exist and have real power. No — that is not polytheism, because none of them is in Yahweh’s class: they are created, answerable to him, and mortal. The texts that sound as if they deny the others exist are claims that Yahweh has no rival, not that he has no company.

## Why ask

If the nations are governed by sons of God who really hold power ([[Deuteronomy 32]], [[Psalm 82]]), the obvious objection is that the Bible is “polytheism with extra steps” [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t1636|27:16]].

## What polytheism would need

Gods who exist independently, co-eternal and co-equal, competing as peers. The council’s members fail every part of that [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t1682|28:02]]:

| Test | The other elohim |
|---|---|
| Uncreated? | No — [[bible::Col.1.16\|Colossians 1:16]]: thrones, dominions, rulers and authorities were created. |
| Equal? | No — Yahweh sits in judgment over them in [[bible::Ps.82.1\|Psalm 82:1]]. |
| Immortal? | No — “like men you shall die” ([[bible::Ps.82.7\|Psalm 82:7]]). |

## The Shema

“Hear, O Israel: the LORD our God, the LORD is one” ([[bible::Deut.6.4|Deuteronomy 6:4]]) is read as “there is only one supernatural being”. The video reads it as a confession of Yahweh’s uniqueness and supremacy — that Israel’s God is the one God in the uncreated, sovereign sense — which says nothing against the existence of lesser beings Deuteronomy itself mentions [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t1727|28:47]].

## “There is none beside me”

Isaiah has God say “there is no other” ([[bible::Isa.45.5|Isaiah 45:5]]). But Babylon says the same of itself — “I am, and there is no one besides me” ([[bible::Isa.47.8|Isaiah 47:8]], [[bible::Isa.47.10|Isaiah 47:10]]) — and so does Nineveh ([[bible::Zeph.2.15|Zephaniah 2:15]]). Nobody takes the cities to mean no other cities exist. The form is a boast of incomparability: nothing in my category matches me [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t1774|29:34]].

Read that way, the denial passages and the council passages stop contradicting each other: **Yahweh is an elohim, but no other elohim is Yahweh** [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t1822|30:22]].
