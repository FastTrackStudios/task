# Sourdough Starter

Flour and water, kept warm and fed, until it doubles predictably. Two
weeks the first time, four days to revive one that has been in the
fridge.

## What worked

- 50g wholemeal, 50g water, room temperature, in a jar with the lid
  resting on rather than screwed down.
- Discard half and feed 50/50 every 24 hours. Twice daily once it
  starts rising.
- Ready when it doubles within 6 hours of a feed, two days running.
  One good day is not the signal.

## What did not

Starting with white flour. It took nine days and never got reliable —
the wild yeast is on the bran. Switching to wholemeal for the first
week and then feeding white works fine.

Chasing the smell. It goes acetone-sharp around day four and that is
normal, not a failure; feeding it more often fixes it within a day.

## Timing

The starter runs on the same logic as everything else slow: the
schedule is the recipe. Fed at 8am, it peaks around 2pm in a warm
kitchen and is past its best by evening.
