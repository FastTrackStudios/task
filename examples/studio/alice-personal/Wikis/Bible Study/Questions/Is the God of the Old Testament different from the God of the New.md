# Is the God of the Old Testament different from the God of the New?

The objection that started the video [[god-told-them-to-kill-everyone-here-s-why-76b70b2b#^t0|0:00]]: an angry, violent God in the Old Testament, a loving one in Jesus. It is old — Marcion made it in the second century and the church rejected it.

## What this wiki has so far

- The conquest is the strongest case for the objection; see [[Did God command genocide in Canaan]].
- The Old Testament describes its God first as “merciful and gracious, slow to anger” ([[bible::Exod.34.6|Exodus 34:6]]).
- Jesus speaks in the same idioms the prophets used, judgment included ([[bible::Matt.24.29|Matthew 24:29]]; see [[Conquest Rhetoric]]).
- The conflict with the powers begun in [[Genesis 6]] ends not in a battle but a cross ([[bible::Col.2.15|Colossians 2:15]]; see [[From Genesis 6 to Goliath]]).
