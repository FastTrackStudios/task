# K. Lawson Younger

Assyriologist and Old Testament scholar. His *Ancient Conquest Accounts: A Study in Ancient Near Eastern and Biblical History Writing* (1990) compares Joshua 9–12 with Egyptian, Assyrian and Hittite campaign reports and shows that total-destruction language is a convention of the genre — the basis of [[Conquest Rhetoric]] [[god-told-them-to-kill-everyone-here-s-why-76b70b2b#^t1971|32:51]].
