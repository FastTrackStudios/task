# Rahab

A Canaanite woman of Jericho who hides Israel’s spies ([[bible::Josh.2.1-Josh.2.21|Joshua 2:1–21]]) and is spared with her whole household when the city falls; she “has lived in Israel to this day” ([[bible::Josh.6.25|Joshua 6:25]]).

In a city under [[Herem|ḥērem]], a Canaanite family survives and is absorbed into Israel — one of the survivors the conquest narrative takes for granted ([[god-told-them-to-kill-everyone-here-s-why-76b70b2b#^t1004|16:44]]; see [[Verbs of Conquest]]).

The New Testament remembers her in Jesus’ genealogy ([[bible::Matt.1.5|Matthew 1:5]]) and among the faithful ([[bible::Heb.11.31|Hebrews 11:31]]).
