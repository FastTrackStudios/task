# The Inheritance of the Nations

“Arise, O God, judge the earth, for you shall inherit all the nations” ([[bible::Ps.82.8|Psalm 82:8]]). The second source calls this the thesis statement of the Bible, and this page follows the thread it pulls: God lost the nations to rebellious powers, and the story is him taking them back [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t46|0:46]].

## Why it was lost from view

Not because it is hidden, the video says, but because we stopped reading with the eyes the authors assumed their readers had. [[Ugarit]] shows what those eyes saw; the Dead Sea Scrolls restored the text of [[Deuteronomy 32]]; Heiser and those before him put it together [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t3511|58:31]].

## Where believers come in

The sons of God of the Old Testament were God’s council, and some of them rebelled. In the New Testament the people of God are called sons of God ([[bible::Rom.8.14|Romans 8:14]]), will judge angels ([[bible::1Cor.6.3|1 Corinthians 6:3]]), and reign with Christ ([[bible::Rev.22.5|Revelation 22:5]]).
