# The Kirta Epic

A Ugaritic poem (also *Keret*; “Curta” in the video’s transcript) from the library of Ugarit, a coastal Syrian city destroyed around 1200 BC and excavated from 1929. Ugaritic is close kin to biblical Hebrew, and its texts share Israel’s divine names — El, Baal, Asherah — and many of its idioms [[god-told-them-to-kill-everyone-here-s-why-76b70b2b#^t2243|37:23]].

## The story

King Kirta has lost his wives and heirs. El appears in a dream: raise an army, march on Udum, and demand King Pabil’s daughter as wife. On the way Kirta vows gold and silver to Asherah. At Udum the army waits six days; on the seventh Pabil, kept awake by his penned animals, gives her up. The dynasty is restored — and Kirta forgets his vow [[god-told-them-to-kill-everyone-here-s-why-76b70b2b#^t2288|38:08]].

## Parallels with Joshua 1–6

| | Kirta | Joshua |
|---|---|---|
| Span | two weeks | two weeks |
| Midpoint | vow at Asherah’s shrine | Jordan crossing and memorial stones |

| Siege | six quiet days, seventh decisive | six circuits, seventh day walls fall ([[bible::Josh.6.3-Josh.6.4\|Joshua 6:3–4]]) |

The same beats, in the same order [[god-told-them-to-kill-everyone-here-s-why-76b70b2b#^t2424|40:24]].

## Not borrowing

Which came first is not settled, and [[bible::1Kgs.20.29-1Kgs.20.30|1 Kings 20:29–30]] has the same seven-day shape without depending on either. The likelier explanation is a shared convention for narrating a siege [[god-told-them-to-kill-everyone-here-s-why-76b70b2b#^t2516|41:56]].

## The differences are the point

- El sends a dream and stays in heaven. Yahweh appears as the commander of his army, sword drawn ([[bible::Josh.5.13-Josh.5.15|Joshua 5:13–15]]).
- Kirta fights for one heir; Joshua for a nation’s inheritance.
- Asherah, bargained with in Kirta, is simply absent; there is no bargaining in Joshua at all.

A Canaanite hearing Joshua would recognise the shape and notice what had changed — a polemic in the same vein as the Baal contests elsewhere [[god-told-them-to-kill-everyone-here-s-why-76b70b2b#^t2653|44:13]].
