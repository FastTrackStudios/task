# Purpose

Alice’s own Bible Study — what she has studied, and what she makes of
it. Context and history for passages she is working through, questions
she has answered for herself, the words she has looked into.

The **Bible Study Library** beside it is the research: sources, their
summaries and a broad sweep of topics, written by an agent. This wiki is
the other half. A library page comes in only when Alice has studied it
enough to stand behind it — “Take into my study” on the page — and from
then on it is hers to rewrite. Everything here is something she has
personally studied.

This wiki is *about* scripture. It is not scripture: the text itself is
the **Bible Resource**, which nothing here writes into. Every page
anchors to verses in that Resource rather than quoting them wholesale,
so the pages keep working whichever translation is installed.

## Scope

In: passages, topics, questions, people and words she has studied —
her conclusions, with her reasons and her sources.

Out: research she has not yet worked through (that is the library’s),
and personal journalling (that stays in the vault).

## Why private

Private rather than unlisted: it is refused to anyone outside the org,
not merely absent from the directory.
