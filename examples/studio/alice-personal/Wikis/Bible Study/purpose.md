# Purpose

Context and history for passages Alice is working through — what the
text meant to its first readers, and how a passage connects to the rest
of the canon.

This wiki is *about* scripture. It is not scripture: the text itself is
the **Bible Resource**, which nothing here writes into. Every page on
this wiki anchors to verses in that Resource rather than quoting them
wholesale, so the pages keep working whichever translation is
installed.

## Scope

In: historical setting, authorship, how a passage has been read,
cross-references worth following.

Out: personal reflection and daily journalling — those stay in the
vault. A page here should be worth someone else reading.

## Why private

Private rather than unlisted: it is refused to anyone outside the org,
not merely absent from the directory.
