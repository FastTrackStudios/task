# Bĕnê hāʾĕlōhîm

“Sons of God”: in Hebrew idiom, members of a class — here the divine one. They present themselves before Yahweh ([[bible::Job.1.6|Job 1:6]]), shout at creation ([[bible::Job.38.7|Job 38:7]]), take human wives ([[bible::Gen.6.2|Genesis 6:2]]), and receive the nations ([[bible::Deut.32.8|Deuteronomy 32:8]], in the Dead Sea Scrolls reading). They are the second tier of the [[Divine Council]] [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t682|11:22]].
