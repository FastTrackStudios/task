# Nāḥāš

The creature of [[bible::Gen.3.1|Genesis 3:1]]. As a noun, *serpent*; the same consonants make the verb *to divine* ([[bible::Gen.44.5|Genesis 44:5]]), and *nĕḥōšet* is bronze.

Heiser reads all three as live in Genesis 3: a serpent, a diviner, a shining one — a divine being in the garden rather than an animal [[what-the-bible-really-says-about-the-other-gods-ac256dcf#^t2140|35:40]]. Most lexicons give only *serpent* here; see [[Three Rebellions]].
