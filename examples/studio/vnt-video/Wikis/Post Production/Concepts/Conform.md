# Conform

Rebuilding the cut from the original footage, at full quality, exactly
where the offline edit put it. Everything downstream is measured against
it: a mix is in sync with a conform, not with "the edit".

## Why it reaches the mix engineer

A conform is where a cut *moves*. Three frames out of a reel is nothing
to look at and is a mix that drifts against picture for the rest of the
scene, so when we re-conform we publish the change here rather than
mentioning it in a reply.

What the engineer needs from us is the change list and the new timecode
start, not the footage — the footage is gigabytes and it never leaves
this building.

## What goes wrong without it

Two suites cutting to two different pictures, each convinced the other
drifted. The version numbers agree, the sync does not, and the first
person to notice is the client.
