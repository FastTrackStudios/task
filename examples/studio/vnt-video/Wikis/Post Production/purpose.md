# Purpose

How VNT finishes a picture, written for the people who hand us sound and
the people we hand a cut back to. This is the one wiki in the example
that lives on **another company's server** — ACME reads it by
subscription, not by being a member here.

## Scope

In: the conform, spotting, loudness delivery, what a rendition has to be
before a client sees it.

Out: colour science and the grading suite's own workflow — that is the
colourist's, and it is not what a mix engineer subscribes for.

## Key questions

- The edit changed after the mix was approved. What has to be re-cut,
  and what can be slipped?
- Why did a mix that measured fine on the desk get rejected by the
  broadcaster's loudness check?

## Why anyone outside VNT reads this

ACME mixes to our picture. When we move a cut, their session is wrong
until somebody tells them — so the two facts that decide their work, the
conform and the delivery spec, are written here once and subscribed to
rather than retyped into an email thread per project.

Unlisted rather than public: it is not a directory entry for strangers to
browse, and anyone we have handed the reference to may take it
(`wiki.access.visibility`).
