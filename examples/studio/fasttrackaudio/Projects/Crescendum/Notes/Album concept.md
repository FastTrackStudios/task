# Album concept

Crescendum is one long swell: every song starts quieter than the one
before it ended, and the record only reaches full level on *Undertow*.

- Overture and Coda are the same motif, a fifth apart.
- First Light is the single.
- The strings on Undertow are recorded with the Rockstars of Tomorrow
  ensemble — which is why their people can read and comment on this
  album without belonging to FastTrackAudio.
