# Ionian

The first mode of the major scale, and the same set of intervals most
people mean when they say "major": whole, whole, half, whole, whole,
whole, half.

Its characteristic tone is the major 7th, a semitone below the octave.
That semitone is what makes Ionian sound resolved rather than
suspended — the ear hears the leading tone pulling home.

## In the room

Ionian is the default a player falls back to when a chart says only
"in C". If a part sounds unexpectedly bright against a track that is
otherwise
[[acme.test/music-theory::Modes@2026-09-01|modal]], Ionian's major 7th
against a flat-7 chord is the usual culprit.
