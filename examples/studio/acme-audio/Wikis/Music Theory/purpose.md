# Purpose

What ACME's engineers need to know about pitch, intervals and harmony to
talk to the musicians they record.

## Scope

In: intervals, modes, the harmonic series, chord function, tuning
systems as they affect tracking.

Out: performance technique, notation software, anything specific to one
session — those belong in the project's own notes.

## Key questions

- Why does a part sound wrong against the track when every note is "in
  the key"?
- What is a producer asking for when they say a mix sounds "muddy" in
  harmonic terms rather than in EQ terms?
