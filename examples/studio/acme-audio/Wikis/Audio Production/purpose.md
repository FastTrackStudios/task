# Purpose

How ACME records, mixes and masters — the engineering practice, written
down so it survives the person who learned it.

## Scope

In: signal chain, microphone technique, EQ and dynamics, monitoring,
delivery formats.

Out: the musical reasons behind a decision — those live in
[[acme.test/music-theory::Harmonic Series@2026-09-01|Music Theory]] —
and anything true only of one session.

## Key questions

- Why does this mix fall apart on small speakers?
- What do we do first when two instruments fight?
