# Mic Placement

Distance sets the ratio of direct sound to room. Angle sets which part
of the instrument you get. Almost everything else is a refinement of
those two.

## Starting positions

- **Acoustic guitar** — 12th fret, a hand's width out. Toward the
  soundhole for body, toward the neck for definition.
- **Guitar amp** — on the cone edge rather than the centre; the centre
  is brighter than the amp sounds in the room.
- **Drum overheads** — equidistant from the snare, measured with a
  cable, or the snare will not sit in the middle.

## Phase before EQ

Two mics on one source will cancel somewhere. Move a mic before you reach for
[[acme.test/audio-production::Equalization@2026-09-01|EQ]] — a
cancellation notch cannot be boosted back, because the energy is not
attenuated, it is gone.

Low-frequency cancellation is the audible one, since the wavelengths
are long enough for a few inches to matter across the whole
[[acme.test/music-theory::Harmonic Series@2026-09-01|fundamental]].
