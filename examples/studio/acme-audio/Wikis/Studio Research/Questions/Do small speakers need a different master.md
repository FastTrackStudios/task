# Do small speakers need a different master

Asked after a mix that held together on the mains fell apart on a phone.
Not settled.

## Where it has got to

The usual answer is "no, fix the mix" — a master that needs a second
version for small speakers is usually hiding a balance problem in the
low mids. But the usual answer predates the current delivery
environment, where the majority of listens really are on one small
transducer, and "fix the mix" starts to mean "mix for the phone", which
is a different claim.
