# Purpose

The agent's own working memory about anything ACME asks it to look into.
Written continuously, by a model, without waiting for anyone to read it.

This wiki is the other half of **Audio Production**. That one is
curated: an engineer has read every page on it, which is the whole
reason anyone trusts it. Keeping it that way means research cannot be
written into it — so research is written *here*, at whatever length and
confidence it actually has, and a page crosses over only when a person
has read it and said so (`task wiki promote`).

## Scope

In: anything worth knowing that nobody has vetted yet. Half-answers,
sweeps of the literature, comparisons that did not go anywhere, open
questions.

Out: nothing. A working memory that refuses material is not a working
memory. What it does not do is *claim* — every page here is provisional
until it is promoted.

## Key questions

- What has already been looked into, so the next pass does not repeat
  it?
- Where did a claim on Audio Production come from, and what was left out
  of it? Every promoted page names its origin here, and the origin keeps
  the working material the curated version had no room for.

## Why private

The pages are unreviewed by construction. Publishing them would put a
model's first draft under ACME's name, which is exactly the confusion
the split exists to prevent.

## Why promotion copies

A promoted page is not moved out of here. Two reasons: the curated page
is the vetted claim while this one is the trail behind it, and deleting
the trail loses the ability to check how the claim was reached; and the
agent reads this wiki on its next pass, so a move would make a subject
look unresearched the moment it became trusted.
