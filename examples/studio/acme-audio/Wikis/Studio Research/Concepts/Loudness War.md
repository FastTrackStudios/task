# Loudness War

The decades-long climb in the average level of commercial releases,
driven by the belief that the louder of two records wins a side-by-side
comparison — which is true, and which stops being useful the moment
every record is equally loud.

## Why it is here and not on Audio Production

This is history and industry context rather than practice. It explains
why a client asks for something, not what an engineer should do. It has
not been promoted, which is the point: a research wiki is allowed to
hold material that is interesting without being actionable.

A page on Audio Production that wants to gesture at it links back here
rather than restating it — which is what promotion does automatically to
any bare link the curated wiki cannot resolve.
