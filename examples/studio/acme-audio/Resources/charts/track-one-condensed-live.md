# Track One

The same song as `chart:track-one`, charted a second way: one verse, one
chorus, no bridge — what the band actually plays on a night when the set
is running long.

**One chart is one arrangement.** These two are not two revisions of one
document that somebody should have merged; they are two readings of
`song:track-one`, which is why both carry that song in their `song` key
and why only one of them carries `is_default`. The original does — ask
for "the chart" of Track One and that is the one you get — and this is
what you reach for by naming its arrangement instead.
