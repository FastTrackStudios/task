# Track One Lights

Ignition's cues for **one song**, which is what `scope: song` means —
`album-launch-show` beside it covers the whole evening. Its cues follow
the sections of `chart:track-one`, and that is the ADR 0003 story in the
planted world: the song is the join, the chart says where the chorus is,
and the lighting says what happens when it arrives. Neither app knows
the other exists; both are reachable from the same `song:track-one`.

`Album Launch Set` — a `Setlist` collection in this org — holds the
song, its chart and this document side by side.
