# Mix review

Half an hour on a rough mix: what is working, what is not, and what to
change before the next pass. Booked by clients against ACME's published
availability.

This is the seeded example of a **bookable event type** — the thing a
booking points at. The bookings app publishes it; a completed booking
against it is what the finance integration bills.
